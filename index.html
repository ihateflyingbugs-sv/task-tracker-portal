Role Protocol

Step-by-Step Workflow Guide

Daily Sequence
Step 1 • Start of Shift
Log Daily Tasks in Batch

Go to My Task → 1. Batch Task Record. Select your assigned Routine or On Demand tasks, attach any reference links, and submit. All new tasks start in Not Started.

Step 2 • During Execution
Track Live Work in Real-time Monitor

Open Real-time Monitor and click Start on the task you are working on. If you need to switch to another priority during the day, click Pause (no note required).

Step 3 • End of Shift
Close or Carry Over Pending Tasks

Click Done once completion criteria are met. If a task continues tomorrow, update its Due Date or select More → Suspend with a brief note.

Step 1 • Morning Audit
Verify Team Logging Compliance

Check Home → Active Team Workload and send the morning WhatsApp reminder if team members have not yet queued their daily tasks.

Step 2 • Live Supervision
Inspect Live Session Timeline

Use Real-time Monitor to view all team activity or filter by a specific member/division. Click any row to inspect deliverable details and attached links.

Step 3 • Reporting & Governance

Review overdue items in Team overview, standardize task scopes in My Task → Scope & Definitions, and export CSV reports from History.

Status Reference & Usage Rules

6 States
Not Started

Task is scheduled for today, but work has not started yet.

In progress

Actively working on the task right now. Start timestamp is logged.

Paused

Temporarily paused during the same day to handle another task. No note required.

Completed

All completion criteria (Definition of Done) are met.

Suspend

Blocked or deferred to a future date. Requires a mandatory reason note.

Cancelled

Logged by mistake or will no longer be executed. Requires a mandatory note.
