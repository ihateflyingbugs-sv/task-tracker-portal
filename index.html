Task Tracker Operational Guide

Role Guidelines

Follow this sequential workflow to ensure timely recording, live execution monitoring, and daily closing.

0️⃣1️⃣
Morning Start: Task Recording

Navigate to My Task → 1. Task Record to log your planned daily activities. Tasks start locked as Not Started by default.

0️⃣2️⃣
Real-time Live Execution

Open Real-time Monitor and click Start to begin. When switching tasks, click Paused (no note required).

0️⃣3️⃣
Workday Closing Protocol

Mark finished tasks as Completed. For multi-day or pending tasks, update the Due Date before leaving.

0️⃣1️⃣
Team Workload Overview

Check Home → Workload Summary to verify team logging compliance and active In Progress task counts.

0️⃣2️⃣
Executive Split View

In Real-time Monitor, select a team member to view their active cards on the left and the complete team timeline log on the right.

0️⃣3️⃣
Reminders & Audit Control

Send WhatsApp group reminders directly from Home and review overdue causes or suspended task notes.

Status Definitions & Operational Meaning

Status Color Code When to Use & Operational Meaning
Not Started ⚪ Not Started Logged and planned for the day, but execution has not begun yet.
In progress 🟡 In progress You are actively working on it right now. Live execution time is accumulated.
Paused 🔵 Paused Temporarily stopped during the same workday. Clicking again resumes In progress. Does not require a note.
Completed 🟢 Completed The task completion criteria are fully met. Remains permanently logged in the daily timeline.
Suspend 🟠 Suspend Blocked by external factors or deferred to continue on a future date. Requires a reason note.
Cancelled 🔴 Cancelled Logged by mistake or will no longer be performed. Requires a mandatory note explaining the cancellation.
