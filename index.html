Task Tracker Operational Guide

Sequential workflow instructions, status definitions, and task criteria.

Role Guidelines
01

Morning Start: Task Recording

Go to My Task → 1. Task Record to log daily tasks. Status starts locked as Not Started.

02

Real-time Live Execution

Open Real-time Monitor and click Start. Click Paused when switching tasks (no note required).

03

Workday Closing Protocol

Mark finished tasks Completed. Update Due Date or select Suspend for multi-day tasks.

01

Team Workload Overview

Check Home → Workload Summary to verify logging compliance and active In Progress counts.

02

Executive Split View

In Real-time Monitor, filter by member or area to inspect active tasks and live timeline logs.

03

Reminders & Audit Control

Send WhatsApp group reminders from Home and audit overdue causes or suspended task notes.
